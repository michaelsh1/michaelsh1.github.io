Frequently Asked Questions

How do I start a quick timer?

Open the Quickstart tab, set SETS, WORK and REST, and tap START. Tap the save button next to it to keep the timer as a saved workout.

How do I create a new workout?

On the Saved tab, tap the + button. Give the workout a name, an icon and a category, then set the number of SETS and the WORK, REST and PREPARE times. Tap Save when you're done.

Can I name each set or give it its own times?

Yes. In the workout editor, open Per-Set Settings. Each set can have its own exercise name, shown on screen during the workout, and its own work and rest times. Drag the handle (three lines) on a named set to reorder it.

Does the timer run in the background?

Yes. Once a workout is running, you can lock your screen or switch apps and the timer keeps going. Sound cues play at each transition.

What does the AI feature do?

In the workout editor, tap Generate Workout. Choose Build to create a workout from your equipment, goals and difficulty, or From Text to paste a workout plan (for example, “3 rounds: burpees 45s, plank 60s, rest 15s”) and turn it into timed sets. Everything runs on your iPhone with Apple Intelligence: nothing is sent to the internet, and it works offline.

Why don't I see the Generate Workout button?

AI generation needs an iPhone that supports Apple Intelligence. If your iPhone supports it but you get a message instead, turn on Apple Intelligence in the iOS Settings app. Right after turning it on, iOS may need a little while to download the model.

How do I change the bell sound?

Go to Settings → Sound to pick the bell, turn the countdown on or off, and set the volume. You can also choose a different bell for a single workout in its editor.

How do I change the app theme or colors?

Go to Settings and choose System, Light or Dark under Appearance. You can also pick the Work, Rest, Prepare and Finish colors under Colors.

My data disappeared after reinstalling. Can I recover it?

InterValue stores all workouts locally on your device. If you uninstall the app, iOS removes its data, and it can't be recovered.
