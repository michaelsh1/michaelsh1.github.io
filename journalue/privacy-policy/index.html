Journalue Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 23, 2026  ·  Last updated: September 23, 2026

Journalue is a private journal. Four promises we keep:
  • Your entries are never used to train AI models.
  • Your data is never sold or shared with advertisers or data brokers.
  • AI reads your entries only to summarise them. Providers that keep prompts are blocked.
  • Delete means delete. Deleting your account removes everything we hold.

What We Collect

Your Journal

  • Entries — the text you write.
  • Photos you attach to entries.
  • Voice memos — the audio recording (WAV) and its transcript.
  • Day summaries written by AI from your entries.
  • Notebooks, places and visits you create or that the app labels for you.

Your Account

You sign in with Google or Apple. We receive your name, email address and a user ID from the sign-in provider. We do not see or store your password.

Location (Optional)

If you allow location access, Journalue uses it to label entries with a place and to detect visits. Visit detection only runs while the app is open and only if you turn on Detect visits. There is no background tracking beyond what you enable. You can turn location off at any time in your device settings.

What We Don't Collect

  • No advertising SDKs.
  • No analytics on what you write.
  • No push notification server. The daily reminder is a local notification scheduled on your device.
  • No access to your contacts or health data.

How It's Used

Running the App

Your data is used only to show you your journal, sync it between your devices, write day summaries, suggest which notebook an entry belongs in, and, if you opt in, add a summary to your Google Calendar. Nothing else.

We never sell your data, never share it with advertisers or data brokers, and never use your entries to train AI models.

AI Processing

Day Summaries and Formatting

To write a day summary, or to format or summarise an entry when you ask, the app sends your entry text, photos and audio through OpenRouter to an AI model provider.

Every request tells OpenRouter to deny data collection. That means only model providers that do not retain or train on prompts are used. Providers that keep prompts are blocked.

Notebook filing suggestions use TypeSafe's Jev model via OpenRouter and receive entry text only.

Voice Transcription

Voice memos are transcribed on your device by the operating system's speech recogniser. The audio file is also saved as an attachment to your entry and may be sent to the AI provider when a day summary is written.

Third Parties

Services We Rely On

  • Clerk — handles sign-in and your account. Clerk privacy policy
  • Convex — stores your entries, attachments and summaries. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest. It is not end-to-end encrypted, so it can be read by the server to provide the features above. Convex privacy policy
  • OpenRouter and its model providers — process entries for AI features, with data collection denied. OpenRouter privacy policy
  • Google Calendar (optional) — if you grant the calendar scope during Google sign-in, Journalue writes one private all-day event per day summary to your primary calendar. Access is brokered by Clerk and used for nothing else. You can revoke it in your Google account settings.
  • Apple and Google sign-in — governed by Apple's and Google's privacy policies.

Retention & Deletion

Delete Means Delete

We keep your data for as long as you have an account. Deleting an entry removes it and its attachments.

Deleting your account in Settings deletes your user record, all entries, attachments and stored files, places, visits, notebooks and day summaries. Calendar events already written to your Google Calendar are not removed; delete those from Google Calendar.

Export is not yet available in the app. Until it is, you can request a copy of your data by email at michaelsh2002@gmail.com.

Children

Not for Children Under 13

Journalue is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

Updates to This Policy

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page and, for meaningful changes, tell you in the app.
