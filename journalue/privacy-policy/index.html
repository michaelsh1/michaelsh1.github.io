Privacy policy

Effective September 23, 2026

  1. Never used for training Not by us, not by the AI provider.
  2. Never sold or shared No ad trackers, no analytics on what you write.
  3. Read only to summarize Providers that keep prompts are blocked.
  4. Delete means delete Entries, photos, voice and summaries, all gone.

Journalue is a journal that writes your day back to you. To do that, an AI has to read your entries. Your data is encrypted in transit and at rest, but not end-to-end. This page says exactly what we hold, who touches it, and how to make it go away.

What we hold

  • Entries. The text you write or dictate.
  • Photos you attach.
  • Voice memos. The audio file and its transcript. Transcription runs on your phone, using the system speech recogniser.
  • Day summaries the AI writes from your entries.
  • Notebooks, places and visits you create or the app labels for you. Location is optional and only used while the app is open, if you turn on Detect visits.
  • Your account. Name, email and an ID from Google or Apple sign-in. We never see your password.

What we don't

  • No advertising SDKs.
  • No analytics on what you write.
  • No push server. The daily reminder is scheduled on your phone.
  • No contacts, no health data.

AI

To write a day summary, or to tidy an entry when you ask, entry text, photos and audio are sent through OpenRouter to a model provider. Every request tells OpenRouter to deny data collection, so only providers that do not retain or train on prompts are used.

Notebook filing suggestions use TypeSafe's Jev model through OpenRouter and see entry text only.

Who else touches it

  • Clerk handles sign-in. Policy
  • Convex stores entries, attachments and summaries, encrypted in transit and at rest. Policy
  • OpenRouter and its model providers process AI requests with data collection denied. Policy
  • Google Calendar, only if you grant it. One private all-day event per summary, written to your primary calendar. Revoke any time in your Google account.
  • Apple and Google sign-in, under Apple's and Google's policies.

Deleting

Deleting an entry removes it and its attachments. Deleting your account in Settings removes your user record, every entry, file, place, visit, notebook and day summary. Events already written to Google Calendar stay there; remove them from Calendar.

Export is not in the app yet. Until it is, email us and we will send you your data.

Children

Journalue is not for children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data. If a child has an account, tell us and we will delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top moves, and for anything meaningful we say so in the app.
