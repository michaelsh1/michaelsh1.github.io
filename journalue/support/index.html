Getting started

What does Journalue do?

Every evening it asks how today went. You talk, type, add a photo or a place. At the end of the day it writes one short summary of your day and, if you connect Google Calendar, files it there so your days are where you already look.

How do I capture something?

Tap + on the journal tab. Pick voice, text, photo, or place. Voice shows a live transcript as you speak; tap Done to save it as an entry.

How do daily summaries work?

Once a day, the AI reads that day's entries and writes one summary. It appears at the top of the day in your journal. If it fails, tap Retry on the card.

Turn summaries on or off in Settings → Summary & calendar.

Calendar

How do I connect Google Calendar?

Go to Settings → Summary & calendar and turn on calendar sync. Google will ask you to allow calendar access. After that, every summary is written as one private all-day event to your primary calendar.

Can I disconnect it later?

Yes. Turn sync off in the same settings screen, or revoke Journalue's access from your Google account. Events already written stay in your calendar until you delete them there.

Voice

Which languages does voice support?

Whatever your phone's speech recogniser supports. Pick the one you speak in Settings → Voice language. Transcription runs on the phone, not on our servers.

The transcript is wrong or empty.

Check the voice language matches what you spoke, and that Journalue has microphone and speech recognition permission in your phone's settings. The audio is kept with the entry either way, and the day summary listens to it directly.

Reminders

How do I change the reminder time?

Settings → Reminder. The reminder is a local notification on your phone; nothing is sent from a server.

Privacy and account

Does an AI read my entries?

Yes, to write your summaries. Only providers that do not keep or train on prompts are used. The full picture is in the privacy policy.

How do I delete my account?

Settings → Privacy & security → Delete account. Everything goes: entries, photos, voice, places, notebooks and summaries.

Can I export my journal?

Not in the app yet. Email us and we will send you your data.

Found a bug?

Journalue is in beta. Tell us what you did, what you expected, and what happened. A screenshot helps. If you are on TestFlight, shaking the phone opens Apple's feedback sheet with a screenshot attached.
